How to use

Open this HTML file directly in your browser. Choose your CAD file, then use the wheel to zoom and drag to pan. Hide unnecessary layers before exporting. The downloaded SVG can be opened in a browser or a vector editor such as Inkscape.

All parsing happens on your device. No server, Node.js, internet connection, or external libraries are required.

Text alignment and fonts are approximate; hatches become outlines, and some curves and object types are simplified or omitted. Review the report before relying on the result.
Conversion report & limitations

The report will list skipped or approximated objects.

    Rendered-element counts include expanded symbols and groups. Approximation counts can overlap. SVG metadata includes these warnings. Colors by layer are generated display colors; original layer colors, line styles, widths, and visibility are not preserved.
